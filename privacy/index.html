Privacy Policy

How Find Antenna handles your information.

What we collect

The current version of Find Antenna does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own, and it does not send anything about you or your use of the app to us or to anyone else.

Your location

Find Antenna asks for your location to work out which transmitters are nearest and which way to point. An approximate location is enough. Your location is used on your phone, while the app is open, and is never stored, logged or sent anywhere: the transmitter list is built into the app, so finding your transmitters needs no connection at all.

What is stored on your phone

Find Antenna remembers two settings: the country you chose (if you chose one instead of Automatic) and the appearance (System, Light or Dark). They are stored in the app's own storage on your phone and nowhere else.

When Find Antenna goes online

Only when you ask it to: Rate opens the App Store or Google Play, Privacy policy and Acknowledgements open these pages in your browser, and the feedback buttons start an email in your mail app. Those apps and websites receive the request as they would from you directly, and their own privacy policies apply.

What we receive from Apple and Google

If you have chosen in your phone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change that in your phone's privacy settings.

If you email us, we use your address and message to answer you.

Changes

This policy describes the current version of Find Antenna. If a later version handles your information differently, this policy and the privacy details on the App Store and Google Play are updated before that version is released.

Contact

Find Antenna is made by Peter Haldbæk in Denmark. Questions about privacy: support@briskbee.com.
