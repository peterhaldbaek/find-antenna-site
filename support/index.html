Help with aiming your TV antenna with Find Antenna.

A transmitter is wrong or missing? Email support@briskbee.com with its name and where you are, roughly.

Something else, or an idea? Email support@briskbee.com. Say which phone you use and what you expected to happen.

Using the compass

  • Hold your phone flat and turn until the needle reaches the dot at the top. The number in the middle says how far is left to turn; 0° means you are pointing at the transmitter.
  • Swipe sideways for the next of the five strongest transmitters.
  • The signal and polarization under the dial say how strong the transmitter is where you are, and whether to mount the antenna flat (horizontal) or upright (vertical).

Common questions

The direction looks wrong. Move away from metal, speakers and magnets, and if the app asks, calibrate the compass by moving the phone in a figure eight. Terrain and buildings also affect reception, so treat the direction as a starting point.

My phone has no compass. Point it north, and the needle shows the transmitter.

I live near a border. Find Antenna picks the country with the strongest signal. To see another country's transmitters, choose it in Settings › Country.

Which countries are covered? Australia, Austria, Canada, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Poland, Sweden, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Does it need a connection? No. The transmitters are built into the app.
